Secure payments

How it works

From connection to internet in four steps.

  1. Connect to Giga.go Wi‑Fi Join the hotspot network at a participating location.
  2. Open the captive portal Your browser shows available packages and prices in Ghana Cedis.
  3. Pay via Paystack Select a package and complete payment with MTN Mobile Money or another available option.
  4. Get online instantly After successful payment, your device is granted access for the purchased time or data bundle.
