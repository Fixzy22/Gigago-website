About Giga.go

We are a Ghanaian-based startup company focused on expanding affordable internet to rural and peri‑urban communities through shared Wi‑Fi hotspots. Our mission is to bridge the digital divide in Ghana by providing affordable internet access to rural and peri‑urban communities. Our services are designed to be accessible to everyone, regardless of their location or income level.

Our mission

Many rural areas in Ghana lack reliable, affordable internet. Giga.go bridges that gap by deploying managed hotspots where people already gather—so students can learn, traders can sell online, and families can stay connected.

How we operate

We install Wi‑Fi equipment at partner locations, run a captive portal for authentication and billing, and monitor network performance to keep connections stable for our users.

Business type: Internet hotspot and connectivity services. We receive payments for time-based and data-based internet access passes sold through our captive portal—not for physical goods, gambling, or cryptocurrency.
