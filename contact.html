Location & coverage

We operate in selected rural and peri‑urban communities across Ghana and expand as new hotspot sites are installed with local partners. We currently operate in the following communities: - Nsesereso (DORMAA) - Danyame (DORMAA) - Kwame Twea Krom (Western North) and still expanding to more communities.

For Paystack business verification: Giga.go sells prepaid internet access (time and data bundles) through community Wi‑Fi hotspots. Payments are collected via our captive portal using Paystack.
